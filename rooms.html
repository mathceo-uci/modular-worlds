Modular Worlds

The labs and the rooms

Below deck, the ship has three labs for learning and seven rooms for making and playing. Each room is named for one thing you do there, and each card says which chapters it goes with. The earliest rooms need nothing beyond Chapter 0.

Keep what you make

Most rooms have a Make a still button. Save your favourite piece and turn it into a card in the Studio’s Gallery for your crew’s show.

The labs

The Proof Lab

Why it is true. Produce proofs step by step like a chemist, with lab reports and Kevin’s casebook of broken proofs.
