The Practice Deck

Before every landing, the crew drills on deck. Each card below makes a fresh problem every time you press New problem, checks your answer instantly, and shows a worked solution when you want one.

How to use it

Aim for a streak of 5 on a card before you move on. Wrong answers are fine: read the solution, then try a new one. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
